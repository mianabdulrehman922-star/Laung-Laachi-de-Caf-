Welcome To Our Cafe

A Warm Sanctuary In Johar Town

Located in the bustling heart of Johar Town, Lahore, Laung Laachi de Café is a welcoming space crafted for those who appreciate authentic Desi tea culture, comforting hot snacks, and delicious modern cafe fare.

From our signature Karak Laung Laachi Chai infused with natural cloves and cardamom to stuffed golden parathas, grilled sandwiches, and loaded fries, every item is prepared fresh with love.
